# A Study System for Busy CS Students

How to handle several modules, coursework deadlines and a group project — and still build things.

## 1. Map the semester in week one

Put **every** assessment into one calendar (Google Calendar, Notion, a spreadsheet — anything):

| Module | Assessment | Weight | Due | Start by | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Example: Databases | Coursework 1 | 40% | 14 Mar | 21 Feb | not started |
| | Exam | 60% | May | | |

- **Start by** = due date minus (estimated effort × 2). Students consistently underestimate.
- Sort by *weight × closeness*: a 60% coursework due in 3 weeks beats a 10% quiz due tomorrow.
- Look for **clashes** now (three deadlines in one week) and start one of them early.

## 2. Plan the week, not the day

Every Sunday, 20 minutes:
1. Look at the calendar for the next two weeks.
2. Pick the **3 most important outcomes** for this week (not tasks — outcomes: "CW1 draft done").
3. Block time for them first, then fit everything else around them.

## 3. Study in a way that sticks

- **Active recall > re-reading.** Close the notes and explain the concept, or do past papers.
- **Spaced repetition.** Revisit a topic after 1 day, 1 week and 1 month (Anki works well for definitions).
- **Code it to learn it.** For algorithms and data structures, implement them, then test them.
- **Teach it.** If you can explain it to a teammate in two minutes, you understand it.
- **Use AI as a tutor, not a ghostwriter.** Ask it to quiz you, explain your error, or give a hint —
  and always follow your module's academic-integrity and AI-use policy. If AI help is allowed,
  declare it the way your module asks.

## 4. Coursework workflow

1. Read the brief and the marking rubric twice. Highlight every "must".
2. Turn the rubric into a checklist and tick it off at the end.
3. Do a rough version early, then improve it. A finished B+ draft beats a perfect half.
4. Version your work in Git (even essays). You'll never lose a file again.
5. Leave a buffer day before the deadline for submission problems.

## 5. Balancing modules, projects and life

- **Protect sleep.** All-nighters cost more than they produce.
- **One side project at a time.** Pick the one that teaches you the most or helps your CV the most.
- **Group-project time is real time.** Put it in the calendar like a lecture.
- **Say no early** rather than flaking late — to teammates, events and yourself.
- **Ask for help early**: lecturers, tutors, seniors, mentors. Office hours exist and are usually empty.
