## When things slip

1. If you're going to miss a deadline, say so **as soon as you know** — not on the day.
2. Missed one deadline: a quick check-in to unblock and re-plan.
3. Missed twice: the team reassigns the task and agrees on a new contribution plan.
4. Ongoing: we raise it with our supervisor/lecturer, with evidence (issues, commits, messages).
