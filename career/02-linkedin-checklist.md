# LinkedIn Profile Checklist

LinkedIn is where recruiters look *after* they see your name — and often *before* they see your CV.

## The top card (what people see first)

- [ ] **Photo:** clear face, plain background, smiling. Doesn't need to be a studio photo.
- [ ] **Banner:** something relevant (your project, a clean design), not the default blue.
- [ ] **Headline** — not just "Student at X". Say what you do and what you want:
      `Computer Science Undergraduate @ IIT | Building full-stack & AI apps | Seeking SE internships`
- [ ] **Location** set correctly (recruiters filter by it).
- [ ] **"Open to work"** set to recruiters only, or publicly if you're actively looking.
- [ ] **Custom URL:** `linkedin.com/in/firstname-lastname`.

## About section (3 short paragraphs)

1. **What you do and care about:** *"I'm a second-year CS student who likes building products people actually use…"*
2. **Proof:** two or three highlights (a project with users, a hackathon win, an internship).
3. **What you're looking for**, and how to reach you.

Write in first person. Keep it human.

## Featured section

Pin 2–4 things: your best project (live link or demo video), a hackathon post, your portfolio, your GitHub.

## Experience, projects and education

- [ ] Same bullet style as your CV (action + what + how + result). Add media: screenshots, demo videos.
- [ ] Add **Projects** with links. Tag teammates — it shows collaboration and grows your network.
- [ ] Education lists relevant modules, societies and roles (IEEE, hackathon organiser, etc.).
- [ ] Add awards, certifications and volunteer roles in their dedicated sections.

## Skills and recommendations

- [ ] Pin your top 3 skills (the ones that match the jobs you want).
- [ ] Ask a lecturer, team lead or internship supervisor for a short recommendation — offer to write one for them too.

## Activity (the part most students skip)

- Post when you finish a project, win/finish a hackathon or learn something: *what you built, one lesson, a thank-you.*
- Comment thoughtfully on posts from engineers and companies you admire.
- Connect with people you meet at events **within 24 hours**, with a short personal note.
- One good post a month beats daily noise.

## Messages to recruiters / engineers (template)

```
Hi <Name>, I'm a 2nd-year CS student at <University>. I really liked <specific thing: their talk,
project, the company's product>. I'm looking for a software engineering internship from <month>,
and I've built <one relevant project, link>. Would you be open to a 15-minute chat, or could you point
me to the right person? Thanks for your time!
```
